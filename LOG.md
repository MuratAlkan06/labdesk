- **decision**: `random.seed(RANDOM_SEED)` is the first statement of `seed_database()` rather than a module-level call, so two calls in one process (as in the determinism test) produce byte-identical data, not just equal row counts. - **decision**: Ticket content, status, assignee, age and resolution time are hand-written per ticket in `TICKET_SEEDS` rather than drawn randomly, so full coverage of categories/priorities/sources/statuses and the uneven workload are guaranteed by construction; randomness only picks each ticket's time of day. A `_check_seed` guard rejects an unknown category/priority/source/status/assignee, a non-Open ticket with no assignee, and any ticket that would resolve in the future — a typo in demo data fails loudly at seed time instead of quietly breaking a filter during the demo.
- **decision**: Five tests beyond the eight the slice specified: notes list oldest-first, `assign_ticket` rejects a non-technician, `get_ticket` returns None for an unknown id, `avg_resolution_hours` is None with nothing resolved, and the seeded data covers every category/priority/source/status. They cover core functions that the eight required tests otherwise left unexercised.
